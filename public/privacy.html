This Privacy Policy explains how RVN Vision, a product of KAILU Labs (Tanuj Shibu, sole proprietor) ("RVN Vision," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and shares information when you use the RVN Vision application and website at app.kailulabs.com (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address through our authentication provider.
  • Fitness and wellness data. Information you enter or generate in the app, including workouts, sets, nutrition and macros, sleep, recovery, body metrics, goals, and preferences.
  • AI coach messages. Messages you send to the in-app AI coach ("Kailu") and the responses generated.
  • Support and feedback. Bug reports, support requests, and any content you choose to share with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data. App events (such as screens viewed, workouts logged, and feature interactions), approximate usage patterns, device type, and diagnostic/error information, collected through our analytics provider.
  • Local storage. Most of your app data is stored locally on your device and, if you sign in, synced to our cloud database so it is available across your devices.

Apple Health (HealthKit) data

If you use the RVN Vision native app on iOS and choose to connect Apple Health, we may read health and fitness data you authorize (such as activity, heart rate, or sleep) to display and personalize your experience. We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it or share it with third parties for their own purposes. You can revoke Health access at any time in your device's Settings.

Payment information

If you make a purchase, payment is processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store your full card number or financial account credentials on our servers.
