Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password or sign in?

Open the app and use the sign-in screen. If you're stuck, email us and we'll help you recover access to your account.

Is my data backed up across devices?

When you sign in, your workouts, macros, sleep, and recovery data sync to the cloud so they're available on your other devices. If you use the app signed out, data is stored only on that device.

How do I connect Apple Health?

In the RVN Vision app on iOS, open the relevant screen and grant Health permissions when prompted. You can change or revoke access anytime in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security → Health.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email hello@kailulabs.com from your account email address and ask us to delete your account. We'll remove your account and associated cloud data within a reasonable time and confirm when it's done.

Is RVN Vision medical advice?

No. RVN Vision is a general fitness and wellness product, including the AI coach. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise or nutrition program. See our Terms of Service.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the in-app bug report option if available, or email us. We read every message and prioritize fixes based on impact.
